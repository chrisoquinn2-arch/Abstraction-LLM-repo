## Motivating questions
How do intelligences discover what an open-ended role requires rather than merely follow enumerated instructions? How do incomplete, ambiguous or changing specifications affect sustained, cooperative research? How might we test executive coordination, feedback, meta-reasoning, self-correction and epistemic refinement without assuming a favored solution?

## Opening concerns, not findings
Observed assistant workflows sometimes seemed to commit prematurely to narrow interpretations, lose context across long interactions, omit duties not explicitly assigned, or fail to distinguish scientific inquiry from planning and governance. These are **unverified hypotheses and anecdotes**, not evidence of a general deficit. Prior approaches might already solve parts of them; novelty must be audited by capability, including what alternatives can do that our methods cannot.

## Working model being explored
Four interacting responsibilities: Governance (ideals, reasons, revision), Executive (information/context movement and accountable cross-domain routing), Operations (infrastructure, feasibility, cost and progress), Research (intellectual inquiry, evidence and interpretation). This is an institutional *design direction*, not a scientific conclusion about cognition or intelligent systems.
