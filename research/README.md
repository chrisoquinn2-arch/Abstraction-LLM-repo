# Research — epistemic responsibility and investigation
[Root](../README.md) · [Charter](../CHARTER.md) · **Stage:** Outset task 1 only. **No research claims have been admitted as findings.**

**Responsibility:** Understand what is intellectually supported, unknown, excluded and possible; formulate questions, methods and proposed experiments; interpret results and propose new research directions. Research may challenge program priorities, but does not unilaterally allocate institution-wide resources or enact governing standards.
