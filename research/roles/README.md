# Research roles — local obligations
**Parent:** [research](../README.md) · **Status:** placeholders only, no research roles activated at outset task 1.
Research stewardship includes meaningful question formation, source assessment, competing explanations, evidence limits, experiment proposals and scientific interpretation. It remains independent of a particular model or human holder; [Executive roles](../../executive/roles/domain-responsibilities.md) coordinate work but do not decide scientific truth.

Future domain roles must record authority, evolving interpretation, active studies, unresolved objections and handoffs; do not establish scientific duties or grants before the relevant outset gates. Challenge missing responsibilities through [Forum](../../forum/README.md).
