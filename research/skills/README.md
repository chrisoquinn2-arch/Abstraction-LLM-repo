# Research skills

[Research](../README.md) · [Governing contract](../../governance/protocols/README.md) · [Runtime](../../operations/institution/README.md)

**Investigate and preserve alternatives.** Load the assigned question, competing hypotheses and underlying evidence by ID. Add proposed experiments, provenance-linked evidence and scoped interpretations as exercise records. Give competing readings separate IDs and use a thread to preserve the disagreement. Propose a supported claim with direct evidence and scope, then seek independent Research review. If a source changes, inspect the reconciliation warning and revisit the interpretation. Scientific recommendations for new priorities go to Executive with grounds. Keep these workflows isolated from real scientific findings during Outset Task 1.
