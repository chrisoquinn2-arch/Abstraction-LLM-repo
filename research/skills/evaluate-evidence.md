# Skill — evaluate claim and prior capability
**Status:** procedural candidate, not scientific result or proof of novelty. **Inputs:** target claim, scope, sources, alternatives, current research stage.

1. State conceptual claim and what evidence would establish or falsify it.
2. Separate observed evidence, interpretation, proposal and speculation. Record source provenance and quality.
3. Compare candidate and prior methods on capabilities each **can and cannot do**, including features prior methods have that the candidate lacks.
4. For a preliminary literature scan, label uncertainty and missing deeper comparisons; do not assert novelty from superficial architectural difference.
5. Identify what the findings establish, narrow, exclude, and leave open *before* advising priorities or experiments.
6. Record adversarial objections, uncertainties and promising counterexamples. Link sources; publish a draft review via Issue/PR rather than silently promoting a claim.

**Stop/hold:** evidence unavailable, authority outside current outset stage, unverified source, or a proposed experiment requiring additional authorization. **Output:** source-linked scoped assessment, not automatic acceptance.
