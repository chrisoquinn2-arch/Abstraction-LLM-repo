# Integrated adversarial exercise suite — proposed execution protocol
**Status:** unexecuted; deliberately requires a human and fresh separate agent holders to test drop-in and recoverability. **Authority:** outset task 1 only. Use synthetic scientific examples; do not populate the accepted research record.

For **every** trial record: baseline commit; initiating actor, actions and source URLs; original state; expected behavior; actual observable behavior; review/person-hour and tool-call costs; failure and correction; who verified it; remaining uncertainty. Compare with a simpler alternative rather than scoring documentation completeness.

1. **Human cold entry:** ask an unfamiliar person to start at root, find a discussion, post a contrary proposal, and locate its status **without using a terminal**. Fail if it requires another database or private chat. Record clicks and confusing steps.
2. **Agent cold entry and restart:** provide only repo URL plus issue ID to a new instance. Recover task, evidence, authority, outstanding objections and next action. Stop original session first.
3. **Wrong-domain / unclassified notice:** create an Issue that initially points to Operations but actually implicates Research and Governance. Executive must detect, route and obtain independent acknowledgments. Fail if the sender must classify correctly.
4. **Authority mutation mid-task:** propose a protocol/delegation change, preserve dissent, record authorized decision, enumerate active dependents, block outdated work and verify reconciliation. PR approval alone must not enact law.
5. **Concurrent edit conflict:** two holders propose contradictory changes to the same research explanation. Preserve both rationale and evidence; resolve through source-linked PR review, not last-writer-wins.
6. **Orphaned role and ownerless thread:** retire/lose a holder. Executive discovers the vacancy and assigns or escalates without assuming every agent holds every duty.
7. **Directory migration:** move a nested resource with inbound links, existing Issue and old handoff. Check references, index/redirect, provenance and cold-start route from old link.
8. **Epistemic uncertainty:** synthetic evidence disconfirms a provisional interpretation. Preserve hypothesis, alternative and what is excluded; avoid treating a forum vote as verified science.
9. **Human review burden:** reviewer receives concise linked packet and must decide whether to accept/reject; note missing grounds and time/cognitive burden.
10. **Failure of participation automation:** disable optional routing/checkers; prove a human submission remains discoverable and no invisible bot is required for an effective manual workflow.

These are capabilities to demonstrate, not a promise that every workflow already works. Capture failures without rewriting history.
