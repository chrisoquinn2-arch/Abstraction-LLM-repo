## Context and authority
- This folder may document critiques of, or proposals contradicting, existing rules.
- Documents here do not override the charter or authorize protocol changes.
- A scientific finding, research portfolio, or accepted knowledge ledger has **not** been created.
- Do not label tabletop cases as empirical verification.
