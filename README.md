# Intelligence and Abstract Identities — Research Program

This repository holds the governing documents and, as the outset work proceeds, the authorized resources for a research program concerned with intelligence under abstract, incomplete, and evolving specifications.

## Founding status

The [Research Program Charter](CHARTER.md) was **ratified by the Research Director on 2026-11-14** (v0.3.1). A rule in Article VII requires reconciliation of subordinate permissions and protocols whenever their source of authority changes.

**Current phase: Article VIII outset task 1 of 5 — establish research and governance protocols.** The initial protocol is a draft pending review. Items 2–5 have not yet been undertaken; no research findings have been formally admitted.

## Documents

- [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) — ratified founding charter.
- [protocols/01-foundational-research-governance.md](protocols/01-foundational-research-governance.md) — proposed governance, evidence, and research standards for outset item 1; **not yet adopted**.

## Sequence

Under the Research Director's instruction, the five outset tasks in Article VIII are to be handled **separately and in order**. Do not initialize the research-resource taxonomy, admit opening research knowledge, evaluate problem-worthiness, or choose substantive investigations ahead of their respective stages.

All consequential edits are versioned in Git. A commit records a proposed or executed edit; it does not, by itself, constitute ratification or approval of a binding policy.
