# Governance protocols

Start at the [Governance index](../README.md). The contract below applies to the isolated prototype authorized for establishment work. [The charter](../../CHARTER.md) remains the sole ratified institution-wide instrument.

## Prototype authority and review

The exercise Director initializes a dedicated ledger. The four seed roles describe responsibilities; activation requires a delegation. Governance owns effective standards and institutional design. Executive owns program priority, dispatch, routing and unresolved responsibility. Operations owns execution, feasibility, recovery and maintenance. Research owns questions, investigations, interpretation and scientific judgment.

Every registered participant can challenge a rule, decision, role, procedure, resource, or the charter through the [forum](../../forum/README.md). A recorded challenge preserves its grounds and its intended recipient. Executive routes it and tracks receipt; the responsible domain addresses it. Close a thread with a reason and preserve its chronology.

Every consequential proposal identifies the change, its grounds, target revision and proposer. A currently authorized reviewer from the responsible domain independently reviews it. An exercise Director applies authorized changes to standards, roles and delegations. Domain maintainers apply changes to their own resources following review. Record rejection with reasons. A proposal concerns its exact payload and revision; after source changes reopen review on the revised proposal. Authority changes invalidate outdated dependent grants and work leases. Reconcile each dependency before relying on it again. No exercise action changes the real charter, adopts these protocols or approves Outset Task 1.

A claim requires current evidence and independent Research review for promotion to supported. A resource linked to an outdated dependency requires review before downstream reliance. A claim challenged by later evidence may remain contested while institutions resolve their own procedural issues.

## Maintenance

Governance maintains this contract and links the evidence for any revision in [decisions](../decisions/README.md). Execution steps belong in [domain skills](../skills/README.md) and in the [runtime guide](../../operations/institution/README.md).
