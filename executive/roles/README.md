# Persistent roles — index and lifecycle
**Parent:** [executive](../README.md) · **Status:** prototype role framework; not a live appointment or charter delegation.

- [Responsibility model and role lifecycle](domain-responsibilities.md) — the four continuing responsibilities, independent of their holders.

Each formal role record should specify: role ID, scope and open-ended mission, authority, current interpretation, active/paused/retired status, current holder or vacancy, active issues, incoming/outgoing interfaces, evidence of performance and revision history.

**No assumption of one agent holding all roles.** Executive dispatch may identify a gap, temporary owner, specialization, merge or retirement; changing a role's binding powers needs appropriate governance. Maintain role indexes on assignment and redefinition and preserve predecessor links.
