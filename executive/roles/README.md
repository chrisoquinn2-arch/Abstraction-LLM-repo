## Role / holder / task / authority are different objects
- **Role:** durable mission with evolving interpretation and continuing obligation to discover missing duties.
- **Holder:** identified human or agent currently interpreting/performing the role; can rotate or be absent.
- **Task:** bounded piece of work, whose Issue records output, status, current holder, blockers and a next action.
- **Authority:** charter or valid delegation permitting actions. Neither holding a role nor a GitHub Issue assignee automatically creates constitutional or domain authority.

A role record should identify its source, scope, mission, interfaces, interpretation, holder/vacancy, active Issues, revision rationale and maintenance owner. This prototype supplies a *candidate* mission, not an appointed worker, active register, or enforceable scheduler.

## Revisions and failures
Role interpretive updates may be needed as new responsibilities are discovered. Binding changes to powers require appropriate governance review. Executive should flag unowned work, overlapping roles and lost handoffs, then request reassignment or role redesign. Never quietly erase predecessor role history. Raise objections in [Issue #10](https://github.com/chrisoquinn2-arch/Abstraction-LLM-repo/issues/10).
