# Role EXE-COORD — Executive coordination stewardship

**Status:** PROPOSED role contract, **vacant / no holder appointed**.  
**Authority source:** [Charter v0.3.1](../../CHARTER.md), Articles V/VII/VIII, and the [four-domain design direction](../../governance/decisions/2026-11-14-four-domain-responsibility-model.md). Neither document delegates a universal right to alter other domains' judgments.  
**Provenance and review:** [Issue #10](https://github.com/chrisoquinn2-arch/Abstraction-LLM-repo/issues/10).  
**Parent index:** [roles](README.md).

## Open-ended mission
Ensure that work, context, challenges, decisions and relevant discoveries travel between institutional roles, retaining provenance, accountable recipients and visible unresolved state. This mission includes identifying **new types of responsibility gaps** rather than merely satisfying a fixed checklist.

## Interfaces and ongoing obligations
- **Governance:** carry requests for review, ambiguity of delegated power and rule contradictions, then preserve authorized dispositions and downstream dependencies.
- **Operations:** solicit feasibility, cost, state, restoration and implementation evidence; notify relevant roles of implementation changes and failures.
- **Research:** route intellectual questions and evidence conflicts without evaluating them as scientifically true merely by scheduling them.
- **Humans and agents:** provide a shared entry and traceable handoffs. A human's forum contribution is not second-class material.

On each assigned coordination Issue: identify *one accountable coordinator*, the receiving role(s), current owner or vacancy, deadline/expected response if agreed, acknowledgment, source references, missing context, the next action, and closure grounds. Preserve cross-domain disagreement rather than forcing false consensus.

## Limitations and failure signals
This is **not** a central authority to decide science or constitutional standards. A single holder can become overloaded; an unassigned issue may be forgotten; wrong-domain routing may go unnoticed. These failures require monitored status/triage and possibly delegated coordinator roles. No automatic monitoring or dispatcher is implemented by this file.

## Succession, reinterpretation and audit
A new holder must be able to find live Issues and historical context from root without prior chat. Role revision should state observed failure, changed interpretation, author, intended effect, affected duties and whether the change alters delegated powers. If so, request a separate Governance review. A reviewer should be able to inspect an actual missing-owner, multi-domain routing and interruption exercise before declaring this role effective.
