# Institutional responsibility contracts (prototype)
**Status:** interpreted design direction; no actual holder or formal delegation is created by this document.

## GOV — Governance stewardship
**Open mission:** Improve and maintain the legitimacy, coherence, revisability and traceability of governing standards. **Success is not a checklist:** newly discovered contradictions, unreviewed objections and unjustified powers create further obligations.
Outputs: decisions, reasons, objections, amendment requests, dependency reconciliation. May not silently ratify constitutional changes.

## EXE — Executive coordination
**Open mission:** Ensure tasks, context and objections reach competent accountable owners, keep cross-domain situational awareness, compare priorities within delegated authority and notice overlooked responsibilities. **Potential failure:** routing monopolizes attention or issues become too numerous; propose subdivision/delegation.
Outputs: routed Issue, owner/acknowledgment, next step and overdue escalation. Does not declare truths.

## OPS — Operations reliability
**Open mission:** Keep infrastructure, workflows, context recovery, cost models and maintenance effective and observable. **Potential failure:** treating a convenient implementation as justification to redefine governance.
Outputs: runbooks, measures, audits, reliable transitions and reports of infeasibility.

## RES — Research understanding
**Open mission:** Investigate meaningful questions, preserve evidence and limits, refine interpretations and propose scientifically significant new directions. **Potential failure:** research has no voice against executive priority.
Outputs: hypotheses, alternative methods, evidence, results, uncertainty and reasoned proposed priorities.

## Holder assignment and change
A role exists without any particular person or model instance. An issue assignment names a *holder*, not the role's entire mandate. Handoff requires a durable issue comment with source links, outstanding disagreements, blockers and next action. Role revision/merge/retirement should be proposed through governance if it changes normative power, otherwise through an authorized role-maintenance PR. Executive owns discovering missing/overlapping owners, not necessarily substantive resolution.
