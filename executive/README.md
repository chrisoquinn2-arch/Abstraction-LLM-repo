# Executive — accountable coordination, not scientific authority
**Parent:** [root](../README.md) · **Authority:** [charter](../CHARTER.md) and [four-domain proposed design](../governance/decisions/2026-11-14-four-domain-responsibility-model.md). **Status:** prototype.

Executive owns *receipt, information transfer, responsibility discovery, dispatch, follow-up, priority interfaces and escalation*. It does not gain the right to declare research findings or amend the charter through routing.

Maintenance: Executive checks unowned notices, overdue threads and disrupted handoffs. Retain routing explanations, owners, next actions and original source links; escalate ambiguous responsibility instead of silently dropping it. Reconsider centralized routing if it becomes a bottleneck.
