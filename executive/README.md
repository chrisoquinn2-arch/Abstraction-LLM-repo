# Executive / Coordination — accountable information transport

**Parent:** [root](../README.md). **Authority:** [Charter v0.3.1](../CHARTER.md), particularly Articles V/VII/VIII. **Status:** proposed prototype responsibility, not a binding delegation or an appointed office.

**Purpose:** Ensure that people and agents can find who is responsible for a concern and that context, tasks and objections reach appropriate roles. This department coordinates without becoming an exclusive scientific, normative or operational judge.

## How to enter and find responsibility
1. From root, identify your task, applicable charter authority, and whether the affected institutional resources are adopted or proposed.
2. To raise a gap or cross-domain concern, open a GitHub Issue describing its target and requested outcome; keep original wording and evidence, even if the domain is uncertain. Agent messages should identify themselves as `[LLM]`.
3. For an unowned issue, the **Executive responsibility is for triage and assignment of a competent role**, not automatic authority to decide the substantive issue.
4. For a cross-domain issue, maintain one source-linked chronology and identify all recipients. Ask receiving roles for acknowledgment and their distinct judgments; do not consider posting alone evidence of delivery.
5. Preserve a next action, owner or visible vacancy, unresolved objection, and reason for disposition. Never equate closing an Issue with ratifying governance or verifying a scientific conclusion.

## Maintenance and revision
The coordinator role may need specialization or multiple holders as workload grows. A role mission persists even if its current holder departs. Treat missing skills, unassigned duties and overdue threads as discoverable concerns, not silent exceptions. Propose any change to **binding** powers through Governance under Charter VII/X. Reassess this directory when hierarchy or shared forum interfaces change and update its parent README and impacted Issue links.

**Unverified:** Agent pickup, notification reliability, automatic dispatch, role assignment and recovery. The scope of this PR is navigation + a role *contract candidate*, not implementation of these mechanisms.
