# Executive routing and responsibility protocol (prototype)
**Status:** trial procedure, not governing law.

1. **Receive:** A new Issue or Markdown dropbox post arrives with an author, target/context, request and time. Executive does not require the author to guess the correct department.
2. **Classify tentatively:** record likely involved domains, kind (notice, question, work, objection, change), urgency, authority and an accountable coordination owner.
3. **Route:** identify responsible domain role and mention/link them in the Issue. If no appropriate role exists, explicitly mark a responsibility gap and initiate role construction/reinterpretation review; preserve the original thread.
4. **Acknowledge:** receiving role confirms receipt and states what judgment it will make. Assignment is not sufficient evidence of a substantive response.
5. **Track:** Executive checks unacknowledged, blocked, overdue and cross-domain items from GitHub Issues. Multiple recipient domains may be linked; each retains its independent judgment.
6. **Resolve or escalate:** final issue comment records reasoned disposition, affected documents, retained objections and next action. Authority changes are referred to Governance; research interpretations to Research; feasibility to Operations.
7. **Audit:** compare Issue/discussion history to document indexes. Detect forgotten threads and stale role assignments; test whether centralized routing itself is overloaded.

**Failure handling:** Lost automation does not erase a human post; Executive manually checks the dropbox/history and files a trackable issue. Wrong-domain notices are reassigned, never silently discarded. Closing an issue does not ratify a law.
